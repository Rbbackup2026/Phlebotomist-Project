Delete your PhleboHub account

PhleboHub accounts are created by your city admin. You can request deletion yourself:

  1. Open the PhleboHub app
  2. Sign in with your registered mobile number
  3. Go to Profile
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm

After deletion you cannot log in. Personal profile data is removed or anonymized. If you cannot open the app, email the support address on the Google Play listing and include your registered mobile number.
