PhleboHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

PhleboHub (Android package com.hsc.mdrctracking) is a workforce application for registered phlebotomists and ambulance drivers. It is not a public consumer diagnosis or treatment app.

Data we collect

  • Name, employee ID, and mobile number (OTP login)
  • Precise location while the app is open (attendance, arrival, on-duty tracking)
  • Photos you capture (samples, bags, odometer, vehicle, profile)
  • Job and trip records required to complete assigned work
  • Push notification token

How we use data

Data is processed on our servers only to operate field work. We do not sell personal data and do not use advertising SDKs. Location is not collected when the app is closed.

Sharing

Data is shared only with your employer/ops team and hosting providers needed to run the service. We do not share data with advertisers.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account in the app: Profile → Delete account. We then deactivate login and remove or anonymize personal profile data. Some operational records may be kept in de-identified form where required for service history.

Web help: Account deletion

Contact

Use the support email on the PhleboHub Google Play listing, or contact your city admin.
